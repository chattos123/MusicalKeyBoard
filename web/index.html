User Guide & Quick Reference

⌨️ QWERTY Keyboard Controls

Play melodies polyphonically directly from your computer keyboard:

ASa / C Wre / C# SRe / D Ega / D# DGa / E Fma / F TMa' / F# GPa / G Ydha / G# HDha / A Uni / A# JNi / B KṠa / C+1

🥁 Percussion Pads Hotkeys

1Dha (Bass Drum / Ge) 2Ta (Snare Drum / Na) 3Tin (Hi-Hat / Tin) 4Jhan (Crash Cymbal)

🎛️ Equalizer & Sargam Notation

  • Select standard curves (Rock, Disco, Indian Classical, Vocal, Bass Boost) or adjust the 5 frequency bands manually to shape the synthesized acoustic profile.
  • Use the Sa (Root) selector to transpose your fundamental tonic across all 12 swaras; scale degrees adapt visually across Mandra (Ṣa), Madhya (Sa), and Taar (Ṡa) Saptaks.
